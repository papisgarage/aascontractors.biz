What We Do

Our Services

From the foundation to the final coat of paint, A&S Contractors delivers quality craftsmanship on every project — commercial or residential.

Structural Framing

Precision wood and metal stud framing for residential homes, commercial buildings, and additions — built to code and to last.

Drywall Installation

Hanging, taping, mudding, and finishing drywall to a flawless surface ready for paint, tile, or any finish material.

Full Buildouts

Complete interior and exterior buildouts from ground-up construction to full gut renovations — we manage every phase.

Driveways

Custom driveway installation and replacement using concrete, asphalt, or pavers — durable finishes for every property style.

Concrete Work

Flatwork, slabs, footings, columns, retaining walls, and decorative concrete — poured with precision and engineered for strength.

Patios & Terraces

Beautifully designed outdoor patios and terraces using concrete, pavers, or stamped finishes to elevate your outdoor living space.

Commercial Construction

Retail fit-outs, office renovations, restaurants, warehouses, and more — delivered on schedule with minimal disruption to business.

Residential Construction

Custom homes, additions, remodels, and renovations built to your vision with quality materials and expert craftsmanship.

Asphalt Paving

Asphalt installation, resurfacing, patching, and seal coating for driveways, parking lots, and roads.

Land Clearing

Site preparation, vegetation removal, grading, and lot clearing to get your property ready for construction or development.

Waterproofing

Interior and exterior waterproofing solutions for foundations, roofs, balconies, and below-grade structures in Florida's wet climate.

Flooring

Tile, LVP, hardwood, epoxy, and polished concrete flooring installation for any room or commercial space.

Interior & Exterior Painting

Commercial and residential painting with quality finishes, surface prep, and lasting results inside and out.

Fencing & Gates

Chain link, wood, aluminum, and vinyl fencing with automatic or manual gate installations for security and curb appeal.

Demolition

Selective interior demolition or full structural teardown — safe, clean, and efficient with proper debris hauling and disposal.

General Repairs & Renovations

Handyman-level repairs to full-scale renovations — we handle punch lists, punch-outs, and everything in between.
